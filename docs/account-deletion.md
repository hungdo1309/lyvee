# LyVee — Request account & data deletion

Use this page to request deletion of your LyVee account and associated data. You do **not** need the app installed to submit a request.

## How to request deletion

Email **lyveelabs@gmail.com** with:

- **Subject:** `LyVee account deletion request`
- The **email address** on your LyVee account
- Optional: your display name

You can also send the same request from the LyVee app: **Settings → Send feedback** (or **Request account deletion**), or by emailing from the address linked to your account.

## What we delete

After we verify the request, we delete account and associated app data, including for example:

- Your user profile (name, email, auth account)
- Group memberships tied to your account
- Items, comments, and other content you created (where it is safe to remove without breaking other members’ shared data)
- Expense participation and related records tied to your account where applicable
- Push notification tokens and device identifiers stored for your account
- Feedback you submitted under that account

Shared household content created by **other** members of a group is not deleted solely because you leave; group owners may still have their own data.

## What we may retain (and why)

We may retain limited information when required for:

- Security, fraud prevention, or abuse investigation
- Legal, tax, or regulatory obligations
- Resolving disputes
- Backup systems for a limited period before permanent purge

Any retained data is kept only as long as needed for those purposes.

## Timing

We aim to complete verified deletion requests within **30 days**. You will receive a confirmation email when the request is received and when deletion is complete (or if we need more information to verify ownership of the account).

## Warning

Account deletion is **permanent**. You will lose access to LyVee groups and content associated with your account. This cannot be undone.
