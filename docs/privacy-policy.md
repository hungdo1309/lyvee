# LyVee Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** July 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LyVee (“we,” “us,” or “our”) — the individual developer publishing LyVee under the name “LyVee Labs” — collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use our mobile applications and related services (the “Service”). By using the Service, you acknowledge this Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the Service.

## 1. Scope

This Policy applies to personal information we process in connection with LyVee. It does not apply to third-party websites, apps, or services that we do not control, including Google when you use Google Sign-In.

## 2. Information we collect

Account information: name, email address, authentication identifiers, and password credentials when you use email sign-in (passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are not readable by us as plain text).

Profile and membership data: groups you belong to, roles (such as owner or member), and invite-related information.

User content: information you create or upload in the Service, such as tasks, calendar items, documents metadata, trip plans, expenses and settlements, comments, and feedback you submit.

Device and technical data: device identifiers used for push notifications (such as push tokens), app version, and basic diagnostic information needed to operate the Service. Usage signals inherent to cloud sync (such as timestamps for creates and updates).

We do not require access to your contacts or photos unless a future feature asks for permission and you grant it.

## 3. How we use information

We use information to: create and manage accounts; provide group sharing and real-time sync; send reminders and activity notifications you enable; operate expense and trip features; respond to feedback and support requests; maintain security and prevent abuse; and improve reliability and features of the Service. We use information only as needed to provide LyVee and related communications.

## 4. How sharing works inside groups

LyVee is designed for shared households and groups. Content you add to a group may be visible to other members of that group based on the Service’s visibility and permission settings. Group owners and managers may invite or remove members. Do not put information in a shared group that you are not comfortable sharing with those members.

## 5. Service providers and subprocessors

We use trusted providers to run the Service, including Google Firebase for authentication, database hosting, and related backend functions, and Expo / Apple / Google infrastructure for app distribution and push delivery where applicable. These providers process data on our instructions to provide their services. When you choose Google Sign-In, Google also processes information under its own policies.

## 6. We do not sell personal information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising as those terms are commonly defined under US state privacy laws. We may disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset transfer, subject to appropriate safeguards.

## 7. Retention

We retain account and content data for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Service. We may retain limited records after deletion when required for security, dispute resolution, legal compliance, or backups, for a period consistent with those purposes.

## 8. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to the nature of the Service, including encrypted transport and provider-managed authentication. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. You can help protect your account by using a strong password and protecting your devices.

## 9. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You can update some account details in Settings, leave groups, and manage notification permissions in your device settings. To request account or data assistance, email lyveelabs@gmail.com or use in-app feedback in Settings. We will respond as required by applicable law. You may also have the right to appeal a denial of a privacy request where local law provides that right.

## 10. Children

LyVee is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us at lyveelabs@gmail.com and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

## 11. International processing

We may process information in the United States and other countries where our service providers operate. If you use the Service from another country, you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in locations that may have different data-protection rules than your own.

## 12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated Policy in the app and revise the “Last updated” date. If changes are material, we will provide additional notice as appropriate. Continued use of the Service after an update means you acknowledge the revised Policy, except where law requires a different form of consent.

## 13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email lyveelabs@gmail.com, or use the in-app feedback option in Settings.
